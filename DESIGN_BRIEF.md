# Design brief: engineering research journal

> Superseded by DESIGN_DIRECTION.md (the scratchbook redesign). Kept for the record.

Written 2026-11-06 after the design interview. This file records the direction for the redesign; `README.md` explains how to edit the site.

## Direction

**A working engineer's journal, printed on paper, with dark figure plates pasted in.**
The page is warm paper with graphite ink. Renders and simulations sit on dark inset plates, the way photographic plates are tipped into a printed journal. Engineering material (CAD projections, schematics, block diagrams) is drawn in ink directly on the paper, on drawing sheets with title blocks. A muted technical blue is used for dimensions, links and figure labels; a red pencil colour is used sparingly for callouts and a few handwritten margin notes.

Audience: technical collaborators and mentors. The page should read like evidence: real figures, real numbers, honest status.

## Type (three families)

| Role | Family | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headings, masthead, figure numbers | Space Grotesk (variable) | Self-hosted, latin subset |
| Body text, captions | Newsreader (variable, 400 to 600) | Self-hosted, latin subset |
| Margin notes (about six, all short) | Caveat 500 | Subset to the characters used |
| Labels, code, data | System monospace (`ui-monospace`, SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas) | Not a downloaded family, so the three-family limit holds |

The interview answer listed a monospace face as a fourth family; IBM Plex Mono was dropped and replaced with the system monospace stack to stay at three.

## Motion plan

Tokens (`--dur-*`, `--ease-*` in CSS; `MOTION` in `js/motion.js`):
`fast 180ms`, `base 260ms`, `reveal 600ms`, `turn 900ms`, ease-out `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`.

Signature moments:
1. **Graphics figure**: Rasterix pipeline, pinned for a short scroll distance on desktop (min-width 1024 px), scrubbing stage and rotation. Buttons always available.
2. **Drawing ink-in**: outlines first (clip reveal, scroll-scrubbed on desktop), then hidden and construction lines, then dimensions and callouts.
3. **Notebook page turn**: StPageFlip curl, 900 ms, explicit input only.

Secondary: headline line rise on load, figure caption fades, fluid frame scrub, gentle parallax on margin notes and figure numbers (desktop only), tab and hover transitions.

Rules: native scrolling; no intro; every auto-moving figure has a pause; loops stop offscreen and on hidden tabs; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables GSAP, pinning, parallax, ink-in and the 3D page turn (instant page change instead). Mobile and tablet get static compositions with every control.

## Assumptions and risks

- StPageFlip 2.0.7 is MIT, about 10 KB gzipped, and has not been published since 2022. It is vendored, loaded only when the notebook nears the viewport, and the notebook falls back to an instant pager or the reading view if it fails.
- In browser testing, StPageFlip's single-page (portrait) mode did not turn reliably (`flipNext` failed when the book was wider than one page, `flipPrev` when it was not). The curl is therefore used only for two-page spreads (book at least 840 px wide); single pages use the built-in pager with buttons, keys, swipe and a short flat transition.
- GSAP 3.12.5 and ScrollTrigger are vendored (standard GSAP licence, free for this use).
- No hardware has been built. Every hardware sheet says "design only" or "conceptual".
- FIRST Robotics Team 1325 and the CodeCraft student count come from the first brief and should be confirmed.

## Implementation order

1. Tokens, grid, type, masthead, static layouts of every section.
2. Graphics figures and drawing sheets with real assets.
3. Notebook: data, generated reading view, page-flip enhancement, fallbacks.
4. Skills matrix, writing, method, about, contact; restyle case studies.
5. Browser verification at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 360 px; reduced motion; keyboard; console.
